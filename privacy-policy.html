Your Privacy Matters: This app stores all your data locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal information on external servers.

Introduction

Welcome to the Pregnancy Tracker App. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about how we handle your information. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect and how it is used.

Information We Collect

Our app collects minimal information necessary to provide you with pregnancy tracking functionality:

All data collected by this app is stored exclusively on your local device. This means:

  • No data is shared with third parties

  • You have complete control over your data

Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device, the security of your information depends on the security measures you have in place for your device (such as device passcode, biometric authentication, and device encryption).

Data Deletion

Third-Party Services

This app does not integrate with, use, or share data with any third-party services, analytics platforms, or advertising networks.

Children's Privacy

This app is designed for use by expectant mothers and does not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13.
