What to Include in Your Message

To help us assist you more efficiently, please include the following information when contacting support:

  • A clear description of your issue or question
  • The device you're using (iPhone, iPad, etc.)
  • Your device's operating system version (if reporting a technical issue)
  • Screenshots (if applicable)

Response Time

As an independent developer, I strive to respond to all support inquiries within 24-48 hours. During weekends or holidays, responses may take slightly longer. Thank you for your patience and understanding.

Privacy

Your privacy is important to us. Any information you share when contacting support will be used solely to address your inquiry and will not be shared with third parties. For more information, please review our Privacy Policy.
